# P-value comparison

- Farther right means a smaller p-value and stronger evidence against no causal effect.
- Right of the dashed line (`p = 0.05`) is nominally significant; left does not prove no effect.
- P-values do not show effect size, direction, or prove causality; also check beta, CI, and method agreement.
- Use adjusted thresholds for multiple testing. Unavailable p-values are not non-significant results.
