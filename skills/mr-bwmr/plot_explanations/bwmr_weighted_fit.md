# BWMR weighted causal fit

This plot places SNP-exposure effects on the x-axis and SNP-outcome effects on the y-axis. Color represents the posterior BWMR weight, and the dashed line is the fitted causal slope.

- Look for whether high-weight instruments broadly align with the fitted slope.
- A small number of low-weight points far from the slope suggests that BWMR is limiting influential outliers.
- Systematic one-sided departures or multiple clusters may indicate directional or correlated pleiotropy that BWMR's balanced-pleiotropy model does not resolve.
