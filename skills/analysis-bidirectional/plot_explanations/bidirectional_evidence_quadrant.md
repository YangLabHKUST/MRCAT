# Bidirectional MR evidence quadrant

Each point is one unordered trait pair. The horizontal and vertical coordinates
are the median cross-method forward and reverse evidence margins,
`log10(alpha / q)`. Zero is the adjusted-evidence threshold. The four regions
therefore summarize Forward only, Reverse only, Bidirectional, or Null evidence.
These labels summarize the submitted MR results and do not prove causal truth.
