# Risk-factor method matrix

Rows are exposures and columns are MR methods. Marker direction and color show
the beta sign, marker size increases with `-log10(p)`, and a black outline marks
a within-method Benjamini-Hochberg q-value below 0.05. The number printed in a
cell is beta. A gray cross marks a method without a completed result for that
exposure. Method agreement is sensitivity evidence, not independent proof of
causality.
