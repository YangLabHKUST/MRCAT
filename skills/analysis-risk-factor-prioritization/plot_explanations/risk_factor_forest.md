# Risk-factor MR forest

Each row is one completed exposure-method estimate for the common outcome. The
left columns separate the exposure label from the MR method. Rows belonging to
the same exposure are grouped together, with alternating light-grey and white
background bands to make adjacent exposure groups distinct.

The MR-method labels are centered in their own column. The point is the
reported beta and the horizontal segment is its reported confidence interval.
Red and blue indicate positive and negative estimates. A filled point means the
within-method Benjamini-Hochberg q-value is below 0.05; an open point means the
q-value is 0.05 or greater, or unavailable. The bottom legend is a single row.
The right columns report the raw P value and number of instrumental variables
(IVs). Compare effect magnitudes only when exposure scales are compatible.
